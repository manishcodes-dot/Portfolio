QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

What technologies do you use? +

I primarily work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript for frontend development, and Flutter for mobile app development. I'm also experienced with Git, REST APIs, and modern development tooling.

What is your development process? +

I follow an agile approach: understand requirements, plan architecture, build iteratively with clean code, test thoroughly, and deploy with attention to performance and user experience.

Are you available for freelance work? +

Yes! I'm always open to new opportunities and collaborations. Feel free to reach out through the contact section below to discuss your project ideas.

 

How long does a project take? +

Timelines vary based on complexity. A simple landing page might take 1-2 weeks, while a full web application can take 4-8 weeks. I provide detailed timelines during project scoping.

Do you offer ongoing support? +

Absolutely. I provide post-launch support and maintenance to ensure your project continues running smoothly. We can discuss ongoing support plans based on your needs.

Can you work with existing codebases? +

Yes, I'm comfortable working with existing projects. I can audit, refactor, add features, or fix bugs in existing codebases with care and attention to maintaining code quality.
